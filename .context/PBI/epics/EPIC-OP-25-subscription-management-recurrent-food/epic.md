# Subscription Management (Recurrent Food)

**Jira Key:** OP-25
**Status:** To Do
**Priority:** HIGH
**Phase:** Core Operations

---

## Epic Description

This epic introduces the capability for users to subscribe to essential food products, enabling automated recurring orders. It allows pet owners to set a frequency for their food replenishment, manage active subscriptions (pause, change frequency, cancel), and receive timely reminders before shipments. This feature aims to increase customer retention and simplify the repetitive purchase of pet food.

**Business Value:**
- Drives customer loyalty and recurring revenue through automated subscriptions.
- Reduces customer churn by ensuring pet owners never run out of essential products.
- Provides predictable demand for inventory planning and logistics.
- Enhances customer convenience by automating routine purchases.

---

## User Stories

1. **OP-26** - As a pet owner, I want to convert an eligible food product into a recurring subscription so that I never run out.
2. **OP-27** - As a pet owner, I want to manage my active subscriptions (pause, change frequency, cancel) so that I adjust them to my needs.
3. **OP-28** - As a pet owner, I want to receive a reminder before the next subscription shipment so that I can confirm or modify it.
4. **OP-29** - As a OnePets operator, I want to view subscriptions and upcoming delivery dates so that I plan inventory and logistics.

**NOTA:** Los IDs serán actualizados cuando se creen las stories en Jira.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Ability to mark eligible food products for recurring subscriptions.
- Users can choose a subscription frequency (e.g., every 2, 4, 6 weeks).
- Users can view and manage their active subscriptions (pause, change frequency, cancel).
- Automated email/notification reminders to users before their next shipment.
- An internal view for operators to monitor all active subscriptions and upcoming orders.

### Out of Scope (Future)
- Flexible delivery dates or specific time slot selection for subscriptions.
- Subscription discounts tied to loyalty programs or tiers.
- Ability to subscribe to non-food products.
- Multi-product subscriptions (e.g., combining food and litter in one subscription).
- Automated payment retries for failed subscription payments.

## Technical Considerations

### Backend
- API endpoints for creating, retrieving, updating, and cancelling subscriptions.
- Scheduled jobs (e.g., cron jobs) to trigger reminders and generate recurring orders based on subscription frequency.
- Logic to link subscriptions to specific products and, optionally, to specific pets.

### Database Schema
**Tables:**
- `subscriptions`: Stores subscription details (user_id, product_id, pet_id, frequency, status, next_delivery_date).

**RLS Policies:**
- RLS policies on `subscriptions` to ensure users can only manage their own subscriptions.
